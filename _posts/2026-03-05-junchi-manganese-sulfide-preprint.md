<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#junchi-chen' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Junchi Chen">Junchi Chen</a> and collaborators, including <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#ray-m-yang' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Ray M. Yang">Ray Yang</a> and <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#manish-kumar' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Manish Kumar">Manish Kumar</a>, released
the preprint [“Equilibrium Thermochemistry and Crystallographic Morphology of Manganese Sulfide Nanocrystals”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05420)
on arXiv. The work investigates the thermochemistry and crystal shapes of
manganese sulfide nanocrystals.
