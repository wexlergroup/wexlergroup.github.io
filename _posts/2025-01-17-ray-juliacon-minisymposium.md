<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#ray-m-yang' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Ray M. Yang">Ray Yang</a> and <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#robert-b-wexler' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Robert B. Wexler">Rob Wexler</a> are co-organizing the Computational Chemistry and
Materials Science Minisymposium at JuliaCon 2025, alongside Letícia Maria
Pequeno Madureira and Rachel Kurchin. JuliaCon will take place in Pittsburgh,
Pennsylvania, July 21–26, 2025.
{: .text-justify}

The minisymposium will bring together researchers using Julia for molecular
and materials modeling. Read the
[organizers' announcement](https://discourse.julialang.org/t/computational-chemistry-and-materials-science-minisymposium-call-for-proposals/124883)
for more information.
{: .text-justify}
