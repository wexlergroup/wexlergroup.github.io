title: "NAIRR Pilot Start-Up Award for Nested Sampling Selection of Fine-Tuning Data"
author_profile: false
---

Our project “Nested Sampling Selection of Fine-Tuning Data for Foundation
Interatomic Potentials” has received a Start-Up award from the
[National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot](https://nairrpilot.org/).
The award provides 2,000 GPU hours on the
[Expanse](https://www.sdsc.edu/systems/expanse/) AI resource at the San Diego
Supercomputer Center. We will use nested sampling, which we have previously
applied to [surface phase diagrams](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4CP00050A), to
select the configurations used to fine-tune foundation interatomic potentials.
