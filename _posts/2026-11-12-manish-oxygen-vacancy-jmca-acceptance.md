<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#manish-kumar' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Manish Kumar">Manish Kumar</a>'s paper
[“Local B-Site Chemistry Controls Oxygen-Vacancy Energetics in Ca–Ce–Ti–Mn Perovskites for Thermochemical Hydrogen Production”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28752)
has been accepted for publication in the *Journal of Materials Chemistry A*.
The work, on which Manish and Natalia Ali share first authorship, examines how
local chemical environments influence oxygen-vacancy energetics in perovskites
for hydrogen production. Congratulations, Manish!
