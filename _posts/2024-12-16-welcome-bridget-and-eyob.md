<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#bridget-ferris' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Bridget Ferris">Bridget Ferris</a> and <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#eyob-tewelde' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Eyob Tewelde">Eyob Tewelde</a> joined the Wexler Group as graduate students.
{: .text-justify}

<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#bridget-ferris' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Bridget Ferris">Bridget Ferris</a> completed her undergraduate studies in chemistry and
mathematics at Austin College. She will develop and apply computational methods
for modeling point defects in materials and studying chemical reactions at
surfaces.
{: .text-justify}

<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#eyob-tewelde' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Eyob Tewelde">Eyob Tewelde</a> earned his undergraduate degree in chemistry from the
University of Chicago. His research will center on simulating and designing
off-stoichiometric, redox-active metal oxide perovskites for thermochemical
water and carbon dioxide splitting.
{: .text-justify}

Welcome, <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#bridget-ferris' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Bridget Ferris">Bridget</a> and <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#eyob-tewelde' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Eyob Tewelde">Eyob</a>!
{: .text-justify}
