Avital Isakov was selected to participate in
the [Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI) Program](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli)
at the [National Renewable Energy Laboratory](https://www.nrel.gov/). Under the
guidance of [Dr. Mai-Anh Ha](https://www.nrel.gov/research/staff/maiahn-ha.html),
she will study poisoning in ionomer-electrolyte catalysts. Congratulations,
Avital!
