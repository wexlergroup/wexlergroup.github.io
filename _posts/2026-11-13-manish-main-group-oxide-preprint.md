<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#manish-kumar' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Manish Kumar">Manish Kumar</a>'s manuscript
[“Periodic Trends in Oxygen Vacancy Formation Energies Across Binary Main-Group Metal Oxides”](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.15297)
has been submitted for publication, and a preprint is now available on arXiv.
The study computes oxygen vacancy formation energies for 39 binary main-group
metal oxides and fits a crystal feature model that estimates them from bulk
properties alone. Coauthors include
<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#bridget-ferris' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Bridget Ferris">Bridget Ferris</a>
and former group member
<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#avital-isakov' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Avital Isakov">Avital Isakov</a>.
