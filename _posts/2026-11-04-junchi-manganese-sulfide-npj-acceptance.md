<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#junchi-chen' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Junchi Chen">Junchi Chen</a>'s paper
[“Equilibrium Thermochemistry and Crystallographic Morphology of Manganese Sulfide Nanocrystals”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05420)
has been accepted for publication in *npj Computational Materials*. The work,
with coauthors including <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#ray-m-yang' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Ray M. Yang">Ray Yang</a> and <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#manish-kumar' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Manish Kumar">Manish Kumar</a>,
investigates the thermochemistry and crystal shapes of manganese sulfide
nanocrystals. Congratulations, Junchi!
