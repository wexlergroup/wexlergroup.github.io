Our paper
[“Accelerating Materials Discovery with Active Thermodynamic Surface Shape Mapping”](https://openreview.net/forum?id=8hSxhqE9f9)
has been accepted at the AI for Accelerated Materials Design (AI4Mat) Workshop
at NeurIPS 2026. The work, a collaboration with Andrew Tipping, Bobby Huggins,
and Roman Garnett that includes <a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#ray-m-yang' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Ray M. Yang">Ray Yang</a>,
introduces an active learning acquisition function that efficiently locates
features such as phase transitions on thermodynamic surfaces, with a test on
phase diagram learning.
