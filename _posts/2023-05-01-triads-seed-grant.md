title: "Wexler Group and Colleagues Receive TRIADS Seed Grant"
author_profile: false

The Wexler Group is one of 10 teams selected for the
[TRIADS](https://triads.wustl.edu/) Seed Grants. The team includes
[Robert Lunde](https://math.wustl.edu/people/robert-lunde) from
[Math/Statistics](https://math.wustl.edu/) and
[Betsy Sinclair](https://polisci.wustl.edu/people/betsy-sinclair) from
[Political Science](https://polisci.wustl.edu/). The project, "Conformal
Prediction for Uncertainty Quantification in Emerging Application Areas, from
Materials to Social Science," will investigate uncertainty quantification in
complex phenomena in the chemical and social sciences.
