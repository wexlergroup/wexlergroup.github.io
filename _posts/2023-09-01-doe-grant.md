---
title: "Wexler Group Receives DOE Grant for Solar Thermochemical Hydrogen Production"
author_profile: false
---

The Department of Energy's Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy
(EERE), through its Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technologies Office (HFTO) program,
awarded the Wexler Group a grant for the project titled "Ca-Ce-Ti-Mn-O-Based
Perovskites for Two-Step Solar Thermochemical Hydrogen Production Cycles."
{: .text-justify}

The project objectives are to optimize Ca-Ce-Ti-Mn-O (CCTM) perovskites for
solar thermochemical hydrogen (STCH) production and to demonstrate on-sun
hydrogen production through a two-step water-splitting cycle.
{: .text-justify}

The Wexler Group will use quantum-mechanical calculations and thermodynamic
modeling to optimize the composition of CCTM perovskites. The work will be
conducted with HydroGEN nodes, whose expertise includes synthesizing and
characterizing these materials under targeted conditions. One milestone is a
hydrogen production rate of one gram per hour using the Arizona State
University collaborators' prototype reactor.
{: .text-justify}

The team will also determine how cation substitution affects the
reduction thermodynamics of CCTM-related perovskites. Collaboration with
HydroGEN nodes will support proof-of-principle demonstrations for these
materials.
{: .text-justify}

The project will include techno-economic analyses of pathways to lower hydrogen
production costs. Collaborators at Ohio State University will lead this
analysis.
{: .text-justify}

The project began in 2023.
{: .text-justify}
