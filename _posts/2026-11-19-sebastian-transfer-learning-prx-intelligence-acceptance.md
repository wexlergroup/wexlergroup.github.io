The paper
[“Transfer Learning on Universal Interatomic Potential Embeddings Improves Generalization in Structure-Property Defect Models”](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003938/v2),
led by Matthew Witman and coauthored by
<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#sebastian-pujet' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Sebastian Pujet">Sebastian Pujet</a>,
has been accepted for publication in *PRX Intelligence*. The study uses
representations from a pretrained interatomic potential to improve predictions
of oxygen-vacancy formation energies. Congratulations, Matt and Sebastian!
