<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#sebastian-pujet' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Sebastian Pujet">Sebastian Pujet</a> coauthored the new preprint
[“Transfer Learning on Universal Interatomic Potential Embeddings Improves Generalization in Structure-Property Defect Models”](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003938/v1),
now available on ChemRxiv. The study uses representations from a pretrained
interatomic potential to improve predictions of oxygen-vacancy formation
energies.
