<a class="person-link" href="{{ '/people/#manish-kumar' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Manish Kumar">Manish Kumar</a> and Natalia Ali share first authorship of the new preprint
[“Local B-Site Chemistry Controls Oxygen-Vacancy Energetics in Ca–Ce–Ti–Mn Perovskites for Thermochemical Hydrogen Production”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28752),
now available on arXiv. The work examines how local chemical environments
influence oxygen-vacancy energetics in perovskites for hydrogen production.
