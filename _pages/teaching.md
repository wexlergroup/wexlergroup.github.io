## CHEM 4050/5050: Computational Problem Solving in the Chemical Sciences

[Website](https://wexlergroup.github.io/comp-prob-solv/){: .btn .btn--inverse}
[Syllabus](../assets/comp-prob-solv/syllabus.pdf){: .btn .btn--inverse}

This course introduces mathematical and computational methods for modeling chemical phenomena at the atomic scale. Topics include mathematical representations of chemical problems, open-source tools for data analysis and visualization, Monte Carlo simulation, molecular dynamics, and analysis of statistical errors. The first lectures introduce Python, so no prior programming experience is required. Students learn to apply these methods to thermodynamic, structural, and dynamic properties.
{: .text-justify}

![Computer in a chemistry laboratory surrounded by molecular models and glassware](../assets/comp-prob-solv/logo.png){: .align-center}

**Semesters**: Fall 2024 and Fall 2025
